# Analysis plan fixed before activation extraction / steering outcomes
Timestamp: 2026-10-02 UTC. Exploratory single-session study, not external preregistration.
Models: Qwen2.5-1.5B and Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct; generation on instruct.
Data: 500 train, 100 validation, 200 test HC3 question pairs, equally allocated across five domains, first qualifying answer per author, >=96 Qwen tokens. Normalize whitespace / punctuation spacing and use exactly first 96 tokens per text for readout. Split by question; exact normalized question deduplication. HAP-E human chunk 2 paired by document with GPT-4o-mini and Llama-3-8B-Instruct: 180 shared documents stratified by six domains, 96 tokens.
Readout: mean residual after blocks 7,14,21, differences of paired means; report single-direction AUROC and L2 logistic probe. Choose intervention layer by validation AUROC of instruct mean direction alone (earliest on tie).
Controls: generate 40 training questions in formal vs casual and assistant vs fictional storyteller styles (same question); estimate mean direction from text-only activations. Also nuisance axes for log original length, likelihood, and domain centroids, computed only on train. Remove their joint span from authorship vector, renormalize to equal norm.
Generation: 60 test questions (12 per domain), greedy <=160 tokens, same prompts across conditions. Add alpha times paired mean-difference vector to all positions at one residual block, including prefill; alpha = -2,-1,-0.5,0,0.5,1,2. Equal-norm Gaussian controls with two random seeds at +/-1; formal +/-1; persona +/-1; nuisance-orthogonalized +/-1; centered projection ablation; human-style prompt baseline. No selection based on detector scores.
Outcomes: independent frozen RoBERTa GPT-2 detector, and blinded Claude Haiku 4.5 ratings of AI-like style (0-100), coherence (1-5), answer adequacy (1-5), and formality (1-5). Judge receives question and candidate only, no condition or reference authorship. Calibrate both on held-out human / machine snippets. Report generation length, repetition and independent base-LM NLL. Bootstrap paired question-level deltas (2000 resamples), exploratory CIs not corrected for multiplicity. Quality-preserving subset requires coherence and adequacy >=4 for both baseline and condition; report selection caveat. Causal claim requires directional detector changes with maintained quality and specificity over controls. Do not interpret a null as proof of no direction.
